MSc Dissertation · Birkbeck University · 2024–Present · Dataset: GSE164416 (36 pancreatic islet donors; T2D, IGT, ND)
  • Designed and built a full end-to-end RNA-seq analysis pipeline handling quality control, normalisation, differential expression (DESeq2, apeglm shrinkage), and co-expression network construction (WGCNA) — producing 30 gene modules and identifying 604 significant differentially expressed genes across T2D vs ND, IGT vs ND, and T2D vs IGT comparisons.

  • Generated 19 publication-quality figures (300 dpi PNG / PDF / SVG) with auto-written methods text, figure legends, and supplementary hub gene cards.
  • Resolved complex environment compatibility issues across Python, R, and macOS to achieve a reproducible, portable installation (conda environment + requirements file).
